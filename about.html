About ITambayan

A digital space created to make learning Information Technology simple, useful, and engaging.

OUR STORY

What is ITambayan?

ITambayan is a student-friendly digital platform designed for Information Technology students. It brings together useful resources, activities, and practical tips in one accessible space.

The website aims to make learning IT less overwhelming by presenting information in a simple and organized way. Whether you are reviewing basic concepts, practicing your skills, or looking for helpful study tips, ITambayan provides a place where you can start.
